PDF Editing FAQs

  • Can I edit PDFs for free?

    With a free account, you can add text, images, highlights, and drawings to your PDFs, as well as organize your documents. Direct PDF text editing requires a Pro subscription, which you can try with a 7-day free trial.

  • Can I get licenses for my whole team?

    Smallpdf is an ideal solution for teams and businesses that need to edit PDF documents and improve their PDF workflows. Our Smallpdf for Teams plan offers tailored solutions for teams of all sizes.

  • Can I edit the text in a PDF?

    Modifying text in your document is simple. Upload your PDF, navigate to the 'Edit Text' tab, and select the text you wish to change. Please note that direct text editing is a feature available with a Pro subscription, which includes a 7-day free trial. You can cancel at any time during the trial if it doesn’t meet your needs.

  • Can I add password protection to my edited PDF?

    To password-protect your edited PDF, navigate to the 'Protect PDF' tool in the 'Tools' dropdown after editing. There, you can set and confirm a password to secure your file for enhanced security.

  • What are the best ways to edit a PDF without Adobe Acrobat?

    Easily edit text, images, and annotations in your documents with online tools like Smallpdf. Forget heavy software—simply upload your file and begin editing.

  • Are online PDF editing tools secure?

    Smallpdf ensures secure PDF editing through TLS encryption, ISO/IEC 27001 certification, and GDPR compliance. Your data is handled safely throughout the editing process and afterwards.

  • How can I edit a PDF on Mac or Chrome?

    You can use Smallpdf’s free and secure PDF editor. Just drag your file into the editor to begin. No installation required.

  • Is it possible to edit text in a scanned PDF?

    Smallpdf's OCR tool allows you to convert scanned documents into editable digital text. Once converted, you can directly edit the text using the PDF Editor.

  • What’s the easiest way to highlight text in a PDF online?

    To highlight sections in your PDF, drag and drop it into the Smallpdf PDF Editor at the top of this page and use the highlighter tool.
